                                                                        Implications. Results from this study indicate mineral
                                                                    precipitation reactions in subsurface sedimentary systems
                                                                    can limit transverse mixing between reactive fluids. Different
                                                                    polymorphs of CaCO3 crystal precipitation can form along
                                                                    transverse mixing zones depending on the solution saturation
                                                                    sate, and these result in a decrease in porosity, a reduction
                                                                    in mixing between reactive species which limits mineral phase
                                                                    transformation, and a reduction in effective permeability.
                                                                    Implications of this study on processes occurring in sub-
                                                                    surface sedimentary systems include the following:(i) along
